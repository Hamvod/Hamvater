NOTEHOW TO READ THIS

A study, not a size promise.

The linked detector estimates the outline of a visible object against a known 5 × 5 cm ArUco marker. Put both in one plane for the marker's scale to be useful. The scan does not understand garment construction, folds, or body dimensions.

Use the result to explore a silhouette. For sizing, compare the garment's own measurements with a reliable size chart and try the piece on when you can.
