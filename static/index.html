ABOUT THIS PREVIEW

An image layer on a model

The male and female forms are original 3D fitting models. Height and weight adjust only their rough display proportions. The garment remains a flat image layer; it does not wrap, drape or stretch like real fabric.

For scan estimates, place the printed 5 × 5 cm ArUco marker beside the whole garment in the same plane. An estimate is not a fit promise. Compare a garment's real measurements with its size chart.
